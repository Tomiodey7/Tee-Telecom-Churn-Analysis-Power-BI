# TEE TELECOM CHURN ANALYSIS - Power BI

**Tools:8** Power BI Desktop, DAX, Power Query

**Overview:** Analyzed 7,000 plus telecom customers with 26.5% churn rate (1,869 churned). Built a 4-page executive dashboard to identify churn drivers and high-risk segments.

**Key Findings:**
- Month-to-month contracts: 42.71% churn vs 2.83% for 2-year
- New customers: 47.44% churn vs 9.51% for 4-6 year customers
- Electronic check: 45.29% churn vs 15.24% credit card 
- Fiber optic: 41.89% churn vs 7.40% with no internet
- Churned customers pay $74 vs $61 retained - $13 higher
- 47% of customers are Critical or High risk (Avg risk score churned 10.08 vs retained 5.11)

**Pages:**
1. OVERVIEW - KPIs and contract / tenure analysis
2. DEEP DIVE - Payment method, internet service, senior citizens, dependants, charges
3. RISK ANALYSIS - Decomposition tree and risk exposure pie chart
4. INSIGHTS - Findings, strategic recommendations, conclusion

**DAX used:** Churn Rate %, Total Customers, Churned Customers, Avg Monthly Charge, Risk Score, Charges Bin

**Business Recommendations:** Focus retention on new, month-to-month, electronic check, fiber optic and high-risk score customers. Incentivize longer contracts and auto-pay.
